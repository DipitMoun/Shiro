# Shiro Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2026-11-14**

Shiro is a free, open-source manga and anime reader, forked from Aniyomi and Mihon, distributed under the GPLv3 license. This document describes what data Shiro handles and how.

## Data stored on your device

- **Library, reading progress, history, preferences** — stored locally only.
- **Downloaded content** — stored locally only.
- **Matrix credentials** (Social feature) — stored locally; used only to authenticate with the Matrix homeserver you choose.

## Data sent to third parties

Shiro contacts the following categories of services **only when you explicitly use the related feature**:

- **Content sources** (manga / anime providers) that you install — the sources decide what they fetch; Shiro relays the requests you trigger.
- **Tracking services** you log into (AniList, MyAnimeList, Kitsu, Shikimori, Bangumi, Simkl, MangaUpdates, Komga, Kavita, Jellyfin, Suwayomi) — to sync your progress.
- **Matrix homeserver** of your choice (Social feature) — to exchange messages with your friends.
- **GitHub** — to fetch extension metadata when you add an extension repository.
- **Telegram** — when you use the Telegram integration with your own credentials.

Shiro does not operate any telemetry, analytics or automatic crash-reporting service.

## Data we never collect

- Advertising identifiers.
- Usage analytics or behavioural tracking.
- Automatic crash reports.
- Contact lists or any personal data uploaded outside the services listed above.
